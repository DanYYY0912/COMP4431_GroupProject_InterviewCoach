Modifications: facial vertex positions and UVs use the project portrait and
locally inferred landmarks, not the canonical face's coordinates. Triangle
winding is reversed for image coordinates; 18 inner-mouth cap triangles are
removed; a portrait-frame mesh is joined at the 36 face-oval vertices.
The original model and SDK are preprocessing tools and are not distributed
as runtime inference dependencies. The portrait is a fictional generated face.
